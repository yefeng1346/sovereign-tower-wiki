## Direct answer: what PC does Sovereign Tower need?

The current Steam listing provides official requirements for Windows and SteamOS/Linux. On Windows, the minimum is Windows 10 with an Intel Core i5-4670K, 4 GB RAM, a GeForce GT 1030 with 2 GB memory, DirectX 12 and 3 GB of available storage. The recommended Windows configuration is Windows 11, an Intel Core i5-9600K, 8 GB RAM, a GTX 1060 with 6 GB VRAM, DirectX 12 and 3 GB of storage.

Steam also lists SteamOS 3.8.10 for both minimum and recommended configurations. The Linux/SteamOS CPU line is Zen 2 4c/8t at 2.4–3.5 GHz, the minimum memory is 4 GB and the recommended memory is 8 GB, and the graphics line is 8 RDNA 2 CUs at 1.6 GHz. Both profiles list 3 GB of storage.
